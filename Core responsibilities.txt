Identity and Access Management Engineer

Hybrid Microsoft Environment
About the Role

This position owns the full identity and access stack in a Microsoft-centric hybrid estate. You design implement and keep identity controls healthy across Microsoft Entra ID on-premises Active Directory and the SaaS applications that connect to them. The work sits at the intersection of architecture and daily operations. Zero Trust is not a slogan here. It is the operating model.

Key Responsibilities

Conditional Access sits at the center of the job. Policies are built tested and tuned so device compliance risk-based sign-in and multifactor authentication actually enforce the principles of never trust always verify and assume breach. Privileged Identity Management receives the same focus. Just-in-time activation access reviews and the clean split between eligible and active assignments for privileged accounts are maintained without exception.

Identity governance runs end to end. Joiner-mover-leaver processes are automated wherever possible. Group-based access assignment is kept current. Periodic access certification campaigns are driven through Entra ID native tools or entitlement management. Hybrid infrastructure does not get ignored. Active Directory organizational units Group Policy replication health and Entra Connect sync are monitored daily. Sync errors orphaned accounts stale permissions and attribute drift are found and fixed before they become incidents.
Privileged accounts are handled through the organization’s PAM platform currently Delinea. Vault onboarding session monitoring and coordinated credential rotation with the security team form part of the regular workload. PowerShell and Microsoft Graph API scripts form the daily automation layer for provisioning deprovisioning reporting and routine identity hygiene. When identity-related security events appear compromised accounts anomalous sign-ins token theft or privilege escalation attempts this role supports the response and escalates cleanly when the scope grows.

Break-glass accounts emergency access protocols and credential rotation schedules stay documented and tested. Architecture decisions exceptions and policy rationale are written down so the knowledge does not leave with any single person. Audit and compliance requests whether SOX ISO 27001 or CISM-aligned frameworks receive timely evidence packages for access reviews and control testing. New applications are onboarded in partnership with application owners covering SSO federation through SAML OIDC or OAuth 2.0 and least-privilege role design from the start.

Required Qualifications

Candidates need at least three years of hands-on identity and access management experience in a Microsoft-centric enterprise setting or a strong two-plus-year track in infrastructure or systems administration that already includes deep Active Directory work and independent PowerShell scripting. Practical understanding of Zero Trust architecture is required not the slide-deck version. Direct experience with Entra ID Conditional Access and Privileged Identity Management is expected along with working knowledge of on-premises Active Directory Group Policy and hybrid sync. Familiarity with authentication and authorization protocols such as SAML OAuth 2.0 OIDC and SCIM is necessary as is comfort with least-privilege models and access certification workflows.
